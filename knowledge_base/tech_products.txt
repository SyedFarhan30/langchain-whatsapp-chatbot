AI Products and Services Knowledge Base

Product: SmartAssist AI
SmartAssist AI is an advanced virtual assistant powered by natural language processing. It can handle customer queries, schedule appointments, send reminders, and integrate with over 100 business applications. The basic plan starts at $29/month and includes up to 1000 conversations. The premium plan is $99/month with unlimited conversations and priority support.

Product: DataVision Analytics
DataVision Analytics is a comprehensive business intelligence platform that uses machine learning to provide actionable insights from your data. It features real-time dashboards, predictive analytics, and custom report generation. Pricing starts at $199/month for small teams (up to 10 users) and scales to $999/month for enterprise plans with advanced features like white-labeling and API access.

Product: CodeGenius IDE
CodeGenius IDE is an intelligent development environment with AI-powered code completion, bug detection, and automatic documentation generation. It supports over 30 programming languages and integrates with popular version control systems. Individual licenses cost $15/month, while team licenses are $12/month per user with a minimum of 5 users.

Product: CloudSecure Pro
CloudSecure Pro is an enterprise-grade security solution that protects cloud infrastructure from cyber threats. It includes real-time threat monitoring, automated vulnerability scanning, compliance reporting, and 24/7 security operations center support. Pricing is customized based on infrastructure size, typically starting at $500/month for small deployments.

Product: VoiceFlow Transcription
VoiceFlow Transcription converts audio and video content into accurate text transcripts using advanced speech recognition. It supports 95+ languages, speaker identification, and timestamp generation. Pay-as-you-go pricing is $0.02 per minute, or subscribe for $49/month to get 3000 minutes included with discounted rates for additional usage.

Support Information:
All products include free email support during business hours. Premium support with phone and chat access is available for an additional $50/month per product. Enterprise customers receive dedicated account managers and custom SLA agreements.

Trial Periods:
Most products offer a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. DataVision Analytics and CloudSecure Pro offer 30-day trials for qualified enterprise customers.

Integration:
All products feature REST APIs and webhook support. Popular integrations include Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier. Custom integrations can be developed with our professional services team.
